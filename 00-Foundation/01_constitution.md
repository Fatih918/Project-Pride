# PRIDE Constitution

**The Permanent Constitution of PRIDE**

---

## Preamble

The PRIDE Constitution defines the permanent principles that govern the project.

Unlike implementation, architecture, programming languages, or artificial intelligence technologies, these principles are intended to remain stable throughout the lifetime of PRIDE.

Every contributor.

Every Steward.

Every organization.

Every future generation of developers.

Is expected to respect this Constitution.

No feature.

No technology.

No commercial opportunity.

Shall take priority over these principles.

---

# Article I — Identity

PRIDE shall always remain an Open Source AI Operating Environment Platform.

Its purpose is to empower people through adaptable technology while preserving freedom, transparency, privacy, and human control.

The identity of PRIDE shall never be redefined to follow temporary technological trends.

---

# Article II — Human Authority

Humans remain the final authority.

Artificial Intelligence exists to assist.

Not to command.

Not to manipulate.

Not to replace human responsibility.

Whenever meaningful uncertainty exists,

PRIDE shall request clarification.

Whenever meaningful impact exists,

PRIDE shall request confirmation.

Human intent shall always take precedence.

---

# Article III — Stable Core

The Core shall remain minimal.

The Core shall remain maintainable.

The Core shall remain modular.

Capabilities that can exist as extensions shall not become part of the Core.

The Core exists to enable evolution, not to contain it.

---

# Article IV — Extensibility

PRIDE shall be designed for extension rather than modification.

Whenever practical,

new capabilities should be introduced through plugins, providers, or external modules instead of altering the Core.

Unlimited Evolution depends upon a stable foundation.

---

# Article V — Openness

The Core of PRIDE shall remain Open Source.

Transparency is a permanent principle.

Community participation is a permanent principle.

Knowledge sharing is a permanent principle.

No future decision shall intentionally transform the Core into proprietary software.

---

# Article VI — Privacy

User privacy is fundamental.

User data belongs to the user.

User memories belong to the user.

User workflows belong to the user.

Cloud services may exist.

They shall never eliminate practical local operation whenever technically feasible.

Privacy shall never become a paid privilege.

---

# Article VII — Transparency

PRIDE shall explain important actions whenever possible.

Users should understand why meaningful decisions are made.

Automation should never become invisible authority.

Transparency creates trust.

Trust creates long-term sustainability.

---

# Article VIII — Community

PRIDE belongs to its community.

Contributions shall be welcomed regardless of nationality, profession, organization, or background.

Ideas shall be evaluated on their merit.

Not by status.

Constructive disagreement strengthens the project.

Respect strengthens the community.

---

# Article IX — Responsibility

Power requires responsibility.

Automation requires accountability.

Artificial Intelligence requires transparency.

Every contributor shares responsibility for preserving the integrity of PRIDE.

No feature shall knowingly compromise user safety, privacy, or freedom.

---

# Article X — Governance

Governance exists to protect the long-term identity of PRIDE.

Stewardship exists to serve the project.

The project does not exist to serve its Stewards.

Stewards are guardians of the Foundation.

Never its owners.

---

# Article XI — Foundation

The Foundation defines the identity of PRIDE.

The Foundation is intended to remain immutable.

Implementation evolves.

Architecture evolves.

Technology evolves.

The Foundation remains.

Corrections that improve clarity without altering meaning are permitted.

Changes that alter the principles established by the Foundation are not.

---

# Article XII — Future Generations

PRIDE is built not only for today's developers,

but also for those who will continue building it decades from now.

Every architectural decision should preserve long-term maintainability.

Every contribution should leave the project stronger than it was found.

The responsibility of one generation is to prepare a stronger foundation for the next.

---

# Final Declaration

This Constitution exists to preserve the identity of PRIDE.

Technology will continue to change.

Communities will continue to grow.

Artificial Intelligence will continue to evolve.

The principles defined here are intended to remain.

Not because they resist progress,

but because they protect the purpose that makes progress meaningful.

No implementation shall be considered successful if it violates this Constitution.

This Constitution stands above implementation.

It exists to ensure that PRIDE remains PRIDE.

Forever.
