# PRIDE Governance

**Stewardship Before Authority. Responsibility Before Recognition.**

---

# Purpose

PRIDE exists to serve humanity through open collaboration.

Its governance exists to protect that purpose.

This document defines how PRIDE is guided, how responsibilities are shared, and how leadership is entrusted within the project.

Governance is not about power.

It is about responsibility.

---

# Our Belief

No individual owns PRIDE.

Not its Founder.

Not its Stewards.

Not its contributors.

PRIDE belongs to the principles defined in its Foundation and to the community that chooses to protect them.

Every leadership role exists to serve the project rather than control it.

---

# Governance Principles

The governance of PRIDE is built upon the following principles:

* Responsibility before authority.
* Transparency before convenience.
* Community before individuals.
* Long-term sustainability before short-term success.
* Principles before personal preference.

Every decision should strengthen the future of PRIDE rather than the position of any individual.

---

# Roles

## Founder

The Founder begins the journey of PRIDE.

The Founder's responsibility is to establish the Foundation, guide the long-term vision, and protect the identity of the project.

The Founder is the first Steward of PRIDE.

The Founder is not above the Foundation.

Like every contributor, the Founder is expected to respect and uphold it.

---

## Core Steward

Core Stewards protect the long-term health of PRIDE.

Their responsibilities include:

* Protecting the Foundation.
* Protecting the architecture.
* Reviewing major technical decisions.
* Mentoring future Stewards.
* Preserving the project's long-term direction.

Core Stewards exist to safeguard the future of PRIDE, not to govern by authority.

---

## Steward

Stewards are trusted members of the community who take responsibility for specific areas of PRIDE.

A Steward may oversee a component such as documentation, plugins, architecture, automation, user experience, or other project areas.

Stewardship is earned through consistent contribution, sound judgment, and commitment to the Foundation.

It is never granted as a reward or status symbol.

---

## Reviewer

Reviewers help maintain quality across the project.

They review contributions, provide constructive feedback, and support Stewards in maintaining high standards.

Reviewers help contributors succeed.

---

## Contributor

Anyone may contribute to PRIDE.

Every contribution is valuable, whether it is code, documentation, design, testing, research, translation, education, or community support.

PRIDE grows because people choose to contribute.

---

# Shared Leadership

Leadership within PRIDE is distributed.

Responsibility should never depend upon a single individual whenever it can reasonably be shared.

Knowledge should be documented.

Processes should be transparent.

Decisions should be understandable.

A healthy project is one that continues to grow even as its contributors change over time.

---

# Decision Making

Major decisions should follow the PRIDE Decision Framework.

Whenever possible, decisions should be made through open discussion and documented reasoning.

Disagreement is expected.

Respect is required.

The objective is not unanimous agreement.

The objective is the best decision for the long-term health of PRIDE.

---

# Accountability

Every leadership role carries accountability.

Stewards are expected to:

* Act with integrity.
* Respect the Foundation.
* Listen to the community.
* Explain significant decisions.
* Accept constructive criticism.
* Place the project's long-term health above personal preference.

Leadership without accountability is incompatible with PRIDE.

---

# Evolution

The people responsible for PRIDE may change.

The technology may change.

The architecture may change.

The community may change.

The Foundation should remain.

PRIDE is designed to outlive any individual.

Its future should never depend upon a single person.

---

# Closing Statement

Stewardship is not a position.

It is a promise.

A promise to protect what others have built.

A promise to leave the project stronger than it was found.

A promise to ensure that future generations inherit a foundation worthy of building upon.
