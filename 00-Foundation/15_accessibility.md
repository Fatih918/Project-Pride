# PRIDE Accessibility

**Technology Should Adapt to People. Not the Other Way Around.**

---

## Purpose

Technology becomes meaningful only when people can use it.

Capability without accessibility limits opportunity.

Complexity should never become a barrier to participation.

The purpose of PRIDE is not only to provide powerful technology,

but also to make that technology approachable for everyone.

---

# 1. People Before Expertise

PRIDE should welcome both beginners and experts.

No person should be excluded simply because they lack technical knowledge.

Technology should reduce barriers,

not create them.

---

# 2. Simplicity Is a Feature

Simple experiences require thoughtful design.

Ease of use should never be mistaken for lack of capability.

PRIDE should strive to make common tasks understandable,

while preserving flexibility for advanced users.

---

# 3. Progressive Complexity

Not every user needs every capability on the first day.

Complexity should be introduced gradually,

allowing people to grow at their own pace.

Advanced functionality should remain available,

without overwhelming new users.

---

# 4. Choice Without Confusion

People should be free to choose how they use PRIDE.

Local models.

Cloud providers.

Official plugins.

Community plugins.

Different choices should coexist within a consistent experience.

Freedom should not require unnecessary complexity.

---

# 5. Accessibility Through Design

Accessibility should be considered from the beginning,

not added later.

Interfaces should communicate clearly.

Actions should be understandable.

People should feel confident,

not intimidated,

when using PRIDE.

---

# 6. Inclusive Participation

Building PRIDE should be accessible to everyone willing to contribute responsibly.

Age,

profession,

education,

experience,

or background

should never determine whether someone is welcome.

Respect for the Foundation is the only common requirement.

---

# 7. Learning Should Be Encouraged

Every interaction should help people become more capable.

Documentation,

examples,

and guidance should empower learning.

Technology becomes more valuable when it enables people to grow independently.

---

# 8. Accessibility Evolves

People change.

Technology changes.

Accessibility should continue improving as new needs emerge,

while remaining faithful to the principles of the Foundation.

---

# Final Reflection

Accessibility is not about making technology smaller.

It is about making opportunity larger.

The purpose of PRIDE is not to reduce capability.

It is to reduce unnecessary barriers,

so that more people can build,

learn,

and create.

Technology serves people.

People should never have to adapt themselves simply to use technology.
