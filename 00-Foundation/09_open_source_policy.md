# PRIDE Open Source Policy

**Open by Principle. Open for Humanity.**

---

# Purpose

PRIDE exists because progress grows through shared knowledge.

Open Source is not a distribution model.

It is a commitment.

A commitment to transparency, collaboration, education, and the belief that technology should remain accessible to everyone.

This policy defines the principles that protect PRIDE as an Open Source project for generations to come.

---

# Our Belief

No single individual, company, or generation should own humanity's progress.

Every technological breakthrough is built upon the knowledge that came before it.

PRIDE exists to continue that tradition.

By remaining open, PRIDE enables others to learn, improve, and create without unnecessary barriers.

---

# Community Before Ownership

PRIDE is founded by individuals.

It is sustained by its community.

No contributor, Steward, Founder, or organization owns PRIDE.

Instead, every participant shares the responsibility of protecting its philosophy, principles, and future.

Leadership exists to serve the community.

Never to control it.

---

# Freedom to Learn

Everyone has the right to understand how PRIDE works.

Its architecture, decisions, and implementation should remain understandable whenever practical.

Education is one of the greatest contributions an Open Source project can offer.

Knowledge shared today becomes innovation tomorrow.

---

# Freedom to Build

Everyone is encouraged to build upon PRIDE.

Whether through plugins, extensions, integrations, research, educational material, or entirely new ideas, innovation should always be welcomed.

PRIDE is not the destination.

It is the foundation from which others are encouraged to create.

---

# Freedom to Improve

Progress requires change.

Ideas should be evaluated by their technical merit, their alignment with the Foundation, and their benefit to humanity.

Not by popularity.

Not by authority.

Not by seniority.

Better ideas should always be welcomed.

---

# Responsible Forking

Forking is a natural part of Open Source.

It is not an act of disloyalty.

It is an expression of freedom.

People should always have the right to explore different ideas, architectures, and visions.

If another project, inspired by PRIDE, one day surpasses PRIDE itself,

then PRIDE has fulfilled its purpose.

Our success is not measured by remaining the only foundation.

It is measured by helping create stronger ones.

---

# Commercial Participation

Commercial participation is welcome.

Businesses, startups, researchers, educational institutions, and independent developers all strengthen the ecosystem when they contribute responsibly.

PRIDE welcomes commercial innovation, provided it respects the project's philosophy, its license, and the freedoms granted to its community.

---

# Transparency

Whenever practical, development should happen openly.

Architecture.

Documentation.

Design decisions.

Issue discussions.

Roadmaps.

Governance.

Transparency creates trust.

Trust creates communities.

Communities create lasting projects.

---

# Shared Responsibility

Every contribution matters.

Code.

Documentation.

Design.

Testing.

Translation.

Ideas.

Bug reports.

Community support.

Open Source thrives because responsibility is shared.

No contribution is too small when it helps others move forward.

---

# Long-Term Commitment

PRIDE is committed to remaining an Open Source project.

Growth should never come at the cost of openness.

Success should never justify secrecy.

The future of PRIDE should always remain understandable, extensible, and accessible to anyone willing to learn and contribute.

---

# Closing Statement

Open Source is more than the way PRIDE is developed.

It is the reason PRIDE can continue evolving beyond any individual, organization, or generation.

PRIDE does not exist to own the future.

PRIDE exists to ensure that everyone has the freedom to help build it.
