# PRIDE Final Declaration

**The Foundation That Guides PRIDE**

---

## Purpose

This Foundation exists to define the principles that guide PRIDE.

It provides continuity across generations of contributors,

while allowing technology,

implementation,

and architecture

to evolve responsibly.

The Foundation is not intended to predict the future.

It exists to provide a stable reference for it.

---

## Responsibility

Every person who contributes to PRIDE shares responsibility for preserving the principles established by this Foundation.

Implementation may differ.

Ideas may evolve.

Technology will inevitably change.

The principles remain the common reference.

---

## Continuity

PRIDE is designed to outlive individual technologies,

individual contributors,

and individual generations.

Its long-term health depends not on a single person,

but on the continued stewardship of its community.

The Foundation exists so that change can happen without losing identity.

---

## Closing Statement

This Foundation is intended to remain stable.

Future decisions should respect it.

Future innovations should build upon it.

Future generations should inherit it with care,

improve it responsibly,

and leave it stronger than they found it.

---

Started by one.

Sustained by many.
